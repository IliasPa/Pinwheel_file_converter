Drag. Hold Shift. Drop.

Pinwheel converts, compresses and edits files on your Mac. Start dragging files, hold ⇧ Shift, and a wheel of formats appears under the pointer. Drop them on one and Pinwheel does the rest. Nothing is uploaded.

Pinwheel Demo

This runs in your browser with the same wheels, wedges and words as the app. No files are read or changed. Keyboard: Tab to a file, Return opens the wheel, the arrow keys choose.

One wheel, the whole job

The wheel shows only what fits the files you're dragging. New files are saved next to the originals and never overwrite anything.

Every format on one wheel

Pictures, video, sound, PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, slides and archives: 44 formats to make, from HEIC to JPEG, MOV to MP4, WAV to MP3 and Word to PDF. Several photos can become one PDF or a collage.

HEIC → JPEGMOV → MP4WAV → MP3DOCX → PDFZIP → folder

The tools, one more key away

Hold ⌥ Option too: Compress, Size Limit (under 10 MB for email, 25 MB for Gmail), Remove BG, an image editor, trimming video and sound, and renaming a hundred files at once with a live preview.

CompressSize LimitRemove BGCropTrimRename

PDFs you can edit

Turn a PDF into Word, Pages or EPUB with its headings, lists, tables and pictures; into Excel with a sheet for each table and numbers as numbers; or into PowerPoint with a slide for each page. Scanned pages are read too.

PDF → WordPDF → ExcelPDF → PowerPointSearchable PDF

Stays on your Mac

Everything happens on your Mac: nothing is uploaded. Undo, at the top of the menu-bar menu, puts the new files in the Trash and brings the originals back. Free and open source.

Get Pinwheel

Free, for Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 26 Tahoe or newer.

Download for Mac View on GitHub
  1. Move it to ApplicationsDouble-click Pinwheel.zip, then drag Pinwheel to your Applications folder and open it.
  2. Allow it oncePinwheel isn't notarized, so macOS asks first. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway.
  3. Let it see ShiftGive it the Accessibility permission when it asks, so it notices Shift while you drag. Then look for the wheel in the menu bar.

Optional, for MP3, MKV, WebM and more, from Homebrew:
